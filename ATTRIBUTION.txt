NLradar - data sources and attribution
======================================

NLradar downloads publicly available data directly from the providers below, on the user's own initiative.
It does not redistribute this data. Please respect each provider's terms and give credit when you publish
images made with NLradar.

- Weather radar data: KNMI (Royal Netherlands Meteorological Institute, own free API key required),
  DWD (Deutscher Wetterdienst, open data), and EUMETNET OPERA / Open Radar Data via MeteoGate
  (CC BY 4.0, with exceptions noted in the data's metadata; data remain the property of the radar operator,
  e.g. RMI/KMI Belgium).
- Freezing-level / temperature profiles: weather data by Open-Meteo.com (https://open-meteo.com),
  licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Free for non-commercial use only.
- Fallback for older dates: radiosonde soundings from the University of Wyoming, Department of
  Atmospheric Science (https://weather.uwyo.edu).
- Background map: the bundled local map tiles. No online map services are used.

This is an unofficial, modified version.
